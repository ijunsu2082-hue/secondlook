Breast cancer · Detection & diagnosis

A second look at breast imaging — earlier, and more clearly.

Breast imaging reports are written for doctors. The wait between a callback and a biopsy result is often spent searching words like “BI-RADS 4B” or “spiculated” at midnight. SecondLook turns the report into plain language and pictures, shows what an ultrasound AI notices, and tells you clearly when a change in your body should be checked.

Biopsy lab for learners

When a needle biopsy samples a lump, a pathologist studies the cells. In the classic Wisconsin study, software measured each cell nucleus from a microscope image. A model reading those 30 measurements reached about 98% accuracy in our tests. Pick a real held-out case and see which measurements push the result toward benign or malignant.
