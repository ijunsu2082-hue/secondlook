For anyone waiting on a breast imaging result

Got a breast imaging result? Let's go through it together.

Breast imaging reports are written for doctors, not for you. Many people end up searching for words like “BI-RADS 4B” or “spiculated” late at night. SecondLook explains the report in plain words, lets you ask questions about it, and helps you get ready for your next visit. It does not replace your doctor.

Ask a question

When you send a question, your message and the report text you pasted go to our server and then to OpenAI, an outside company, so it can write an answer. Our server does not store them, but OpenAI may keep them for a short time under its own rules. Please do not include your name, birth date or other details that identify you. The assistant can make mistakes. It cannot diagnose you.

Biopsy lab for learning

When a needle biopsy takes a sample from a lump, a doctor called a pathologist looks at the cells. In a well known study from Wisconsin, software measured the center of each cell (the nucleus) in microscope images. Our small model reads those 30 measurements. Pick a real example it has not seen before and see which measurements push the answer toward benign (not cancer) or malignant (cancer).

How it works

This is not medical advice. SecondLook is a learning tool made for the Code the Cure hackathon. If you notice a change in your breast, contact a doctor. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Reports, images, symptoms and lab sliders are handled in your browser and are not sent anywhere. The one exception is the chat: your question and report text go to our server and to OpenAI to get an answer.
